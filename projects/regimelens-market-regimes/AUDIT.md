# RegimeLens — Data & Information-Boundary Audit

## Version mismatch

The report-derived case describes five regimes and 2021–2026, with Macro F1 0.563 and simulated Sharpe 0.90 versus 0.48. The recovered `mode_test_.ipynb` uses three HMM states on 2000–2026 source data. Its saved classification scores near 0.94 concern next-day labels generated by that HMM. These are different experiment definitions; no parity or improvement comparison is claimed.

## Historical code findings

- `.groupby('ticker')[...].ffill().bfill()` backfills after the grouped operation, potentially crossing ticker boundaries as well as bringing future observations backwards.
- StandardScaler and GaussianHMM are fit on the full sequence before the 70/15/15 split. Viterbi-style labels can also use later observations.
- A predictor of persistent latent labels needs a persistence baseline. High classification scores alone do not establish useful investment signals.

## Fresh protocol

The current experiment uses the original CSV snapshot, 2,087,863 stock rows, and 6,374 market days. Prices/volumes are checked, missing/invalid rows dropped, per-ticker historical returns/rolling averages computed, then market aggregates formed. VIX is joined backwards by observed date. No backfill or cross-ticker imputation.

The first 70% of days fit StandardScaler and a three-state diagonal-covariance GaussianHMM (seed 42, max 200 iterations). A log-space forward recursion computes p(z_t | x_1…x_t). The next 15% selects among persistence, Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost by macro F1; the final 15% is the reported test. Boundary pairs whose t+1 label would enter the next period are removed. The HMM is not refit after selection. The future-perturbation check and three hand/behavior tests pass.

## Remaining limits

Fixed source constituents can have survivorship bias; no historical membership reconstruction. Adjusted prices may encode later corporate actions. A same-date VIX join assumes end-of-day availability and does not reconstruct release vintages. Labels are endogenous to a fitted HMM, not independent economic annotations. One split/seed, no external validation, multi-window refitting, SHAP, trading-cost analysis or backtest. The fresh metrics describe this bounded classification task only.
